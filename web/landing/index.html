Before you choose.

Do I need to download an app?

No. This prototype runs in your browser. It does not monitor regular mobile calls; a real calling route still needs to be connected.

Can I choose from my contacts?

If your browser supports a native contact picker, you can open it and choose one person. Support varies. First name and mobile number fields are always available. Details stay in the open setup dialog and are cleared when you finish or close it.

What can my trusted person hear?

Nothing here: this prototype does not connect calls. In the proposed product, your trusted person would hear the ongoing call after answering and accepting the invitation, not before joining.

What if they do not answer?

They do not join. Keep your code private and hang up if you’re unsure. Call your bank using the number on your card.

Is my phone protected after setup?

No. This is only a setup prototype. It does not monitor calls, send invitations, or place calls. No contact details are sent to or stored on a server.
